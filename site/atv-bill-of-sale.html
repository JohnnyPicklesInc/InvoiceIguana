ATV bill of sale FAQ

What should an ATV bill of sale include?

Seller and buyer, the ATV/UTV (year, make, model, VIN, hours or mileage), price, date, payment, an as-is clause, and signatures. Hours matter more than mileage for many off-road machines.

Do ATVs need a title or bill of sale?

Titling rules vary by state; many require a bill of sale for registration or trail permits even when a title isn't issued. Check your state.

Should I note "off-road use only"?

If relevant, add it to the terms so the buyer understands the machine isn't street-legal.
