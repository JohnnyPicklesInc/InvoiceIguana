Junk Removal invoice FAQ

What should a junk removal invoice include?

Your business name, the customer and pickup address, the load size or volume, disposal/dump fees, the date, tax, and total. Note any heavy or hazardous items that carried a surcharge.

How is junk removal priced?

By volume — fractions of a truck load (¼, ½, full) — plus disposal fees. Surcharges apply to appliances, mattresses, and e-waste. Add those as line items.

Should dump fees be a separate line?

Passing disposal fees through as their own line explains the total and is fair when landfill costs vary by material.
