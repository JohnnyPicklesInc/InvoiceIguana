Pressure Washing invoice FAQ

What should a pressure washing invoice include?

Your business name, the customer and service address, each surface cleaned as a line item, the service date, tax if applicable, and total. Note the method (soft wash vs. high pressure) for delicate surfaces.

Should pressure washing be billed per job or per square foot?

Residential is usually flat per surface (house, driveway, deck); large commercial flatwork is often per square foot. List each area separately either way.

How do I invoice recurring commercial cleaning?

Bill per visit or monthly. Save the client and duplicate the last invoice to reuse surfaces and rates — just update the date.
