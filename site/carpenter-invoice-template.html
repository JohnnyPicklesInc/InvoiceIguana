Carpenter invoice FAQ

What should a carpentry invoice include?

Business name, customer and job address, a description of the piece or work, materials (lumber, hardware, finish), labor hours, any deposit, tax, and the balance due. Photos or a drawing reference can go in the notes.

How do I invoice a custom carpentry job with a deposit?

Bill the full project price, then subtract the deposit already paid so the invoice shows the remaining balance. Note the deposit date for your records.

Should materials be a separate line from labor?

Yes — separating lumber and hardware from shop/finishing labor makes custom quotes easy to understand and easier to approve.
