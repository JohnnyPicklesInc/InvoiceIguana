Concrete quote & estimate FAQ

What should a concrete estimate include?

Your business, the property, the square footage and thickness, excavation and base, reinforcement, finish type, the valid-until date, deposit, and total. Note the cure time and any weather contingencies.

Is concrete quoted per square foot?

Yes — a per-square-foot poured-and-finished rate, plus separate lines for excavation, base, and reinforcement, which vary by site.

How long is a concrete quote valid?

About 30 days — concrete and fuel prices move. A valid-until date keeps the number current.
