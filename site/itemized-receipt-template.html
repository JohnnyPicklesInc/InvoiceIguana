Itemized receipt FAQ

What makes a receipt "itemized"?

It lists each item or service separately with its price, then shows subtotal, tax, and total — rather than a single lump sum. Itemization is what reimbursement, HSA/FSA, and tax records require.

Why do I need an itemized receipt for reimbursement?

Employers and benefit accounts need to see exactly what was purchased, not just a total, to confirm it’s eligible. A card slip alone usually isn’t enough.

How do I make an itemized receipt?

Add each item as its own line with quantity and price, set the tax, and the generator totals it. Download a PDF or share a link — no signup.
