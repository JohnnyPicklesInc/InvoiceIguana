Pest Control invoice FAQ

What should a pest control invoice include?

Your company and applicator license, the customer and property address, the treatment or plan, target pests, areas treated, the service date, tax, and total. Note any re-service guarantee.

How do I invoice a recurring pest control plan?

Bill the initial treatment, then each recurring visit (monthly or quarterly) as its own invoice. Save the client and duplicate to rebill each cycle.

Should the target pest be on the invoice?

Yes — a short note (ants, roaches, rodents, termites) documents the scope and helps with warranty or callback questions.
