Photographer invoice FAQ

What should a photography invoice include?

Your business name, the client, the shoot date and type, deliverables (hours, edited images, gallery, prints), any retainer already paid, usage/licensing terms, total, and the balance due. Put licensing details in the notes.

How do I invoice a photography retainer or deposit?

Bill the full package, then subtract the retainer so the invoice shows the remaining balance. Note the retainer amount and date — retainers are often non-refundable, so say so in the terms.

Should image licensing be on the invoice?

For commercial or event work, yes — a short usage line (personal use, print rights, or commercial license) in the notes sets expectations and protects your work.
