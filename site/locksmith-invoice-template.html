Locksmith invoice FAQ

What should a locksmith invoice include?

Your business and license, the customer and location, the service call, hardware/parts, labor, tax, and total. For automotive or commercial work, note the vehicle or door count.

Should a locksmith charge a service-call fee?

Most do — a flat trip/service fee that may or may not roll into the job. Show it as its own line so the invoice is transparent.

How do I invoice a lockout vs. a rekey?

A lockout is usually a single flat line plus the service call; a rekey itemizes cylinders and any new hardware. This template does both.
