Web Developer invoice FAQ

What should a web development invoice include?

Your name or agency, the client, the project or milestone, itemized build/scope/hours, any pass-through costs (hosting, domain, plugins), deposit, total, and terms. Reference the proposal or SOW for context.

How do I invoice web development milestones?

Split the project into milestones (deposit, design approved, launch) and bill each as its own invoice. Note the total contract value and amount billed to date so the client can track it.

How should I handle hosting or plugin costs on the invoice?

Pass them through as their own line items (e.g. "Annual hosting"), separate from your labor, so it is clear which costs are third-party.
