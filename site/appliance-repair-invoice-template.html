Appliance Repair invoice FAQ

What should an appliance repair invoice include?

Your business name, the customer, the appliance (make/model helps), the diagnostic fee, parts, labor, tax, total, and any parts/labor warranty. A short problem description avoids confusion.

Do you charge a diagnostic fee separately?

Usually yes — a flat diagnostic that may be waived if the customer proceeds. Show it as a line item; the generator lets you discount it if you roll it in.

Should the part number be on the invoice?

For warranty claims, yes — add the part and its number to the line or the notes so the customer has it on record.
