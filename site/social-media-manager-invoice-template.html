Social Media Manager invoice FAQ

What should a social media invoice include?

Your name or agency, the client, the billing month, the retainer and deliverables (posts, platforms, reporting), any ad spend passed through, total, and terms.

How do I handle ad spend on the invoice?

Pass it through as its own line item, separate from your management fee, so it’s clear which cost is the platform’s and which is yours.

How do I bill a monthly retainer?

One recurring line for the retainer plus any extras. Save the client and duplicate last month’s invoice to rebill in seconds.
