Motorcycle bill of sale FAQ

What should a motorcycle bill of sale include?

Seller and buyer, the bike (year, make, model, VIN, engine size, colour, mileage), price, date, payment, an as-is clause, and signatures. VIN and mileage are the key identifiers.

Does a motorcycle bill of sale need a witness or notary?

Some states require a notary or witness for vehicle transfers, others don't. Check your state DMV; the template leaves signature lines for both parties (and a witness if you want one).

Can I use this if the title is missing?

You can still record the sale, but the buyer will usually need the title (or a replacement) to register the bike. Note the title status in the terms.
