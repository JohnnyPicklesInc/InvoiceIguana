Rent receipt FAQ

What should a rent receipt include?

The landlord or property name, the tenant, the amount paid, the rental period (month), the date received, the payment method, and the remaining balance if any. A receipt number helps you track it.

Are landlords required to give rent receipts?

In many states, yes — especially for cash payments or on request. Even where it’s optional, issuing receipts protects both sides. Check your local landlord-tenant rules.

How do I write a rent receipt?

Enter your property as the "merchant", add a line like "Rent — [month]", set the amount and payment method, and note zero balance. Save the tenant to reissue quickly next month.
