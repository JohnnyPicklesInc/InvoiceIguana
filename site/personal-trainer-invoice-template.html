Personal Trainer invoice FAQ

What should a personal training invoice include?

Your name or business, the client, the package or number of sessions, the rate, the billing period, total, and payment terms. For packages, note how many sessions remain if you track that.

How do trainers usually bill — per session or per package?

Packages (e.g. 10 sessions) are most common because they lock in commitment and simplify billing. Per-session or monthly retainers also work; list them as line items.

Can I use this for online coaching invoices?

Yes — swap the session package for a monthly coaching or programming line. Save the client and duplicate the invoice each month to rebill quickly.
