Boat bill of sale FAQ

What should a boat bill of sale include?

Seller and buyer, the boat (year, make, model, length, hull ID number/HIN, motor), the trailer if included, price, date, payment, an as-is clause, and signatures. The HIN is the boat's equivalent of a VIN.

Is the trailer included in a boat bill of sale?

Only if you say so — a trailer is often a separate titled item. List it (with its VIN) in the description if it's part of the sale, or do a separate bill of sale for it.

Do I need to register the boat after buying it?

Usually yes, with your state's boating/DMV agency, and the bill of sale is often required. Requirements vary by state and boat size — check locally.
