Restaurant receipt FAQ

What should a restaurant receipt include?

The restaurant name, the date, itemized food and drinks, subtotal, tax, tip, total, and the payment method. Diners often need the itemized version for expense reports or reimbursement.

How do I add a tip to a receipt?

A tip is a flat amount added after tax. In the generator, enter the tip amount and the total updates automatically — the receipt total already includes it.

Do I need an itemized receipt for a business meal?

For expense reports and taxes, an itemized receipt (not just the card slip) is usually required. This template itemizes each item, tax, and tip.
