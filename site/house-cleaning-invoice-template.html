House Cleaning invoice FAQ

What should a house cleaning invoice include?

Your name or business, the client and service address, the clean type (standard, deep, move-out), any add-ons, the date of service, total, and payment terms. A recurring schedule note helps for weekly or biweekly clients.

Should cleaners charge by the hour or by the job?

Flat per-job pricing is most common for residential (clients like knowing the price up front); hourly suits one-off deep cleans. List add-ons like ovens or windows as separate lines either way.

Do house cleaners charge sales tax?

Cleaning services are taxable in some states and not others. This example uses 0% — set your local rate in the generator if services are taxable where you work.
