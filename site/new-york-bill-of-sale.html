New York bill of sale FAQ

What does a New York bill of sale need?

Seller and buyer, the item (vehicle: year, make, model, VIN, odometer), price, date, payment, an as-is clause, and signatures. New York's DMV provides its own bill of sale (MV-912) — check dmv.ny.gov for title and sales-tax forms.

Do I need the DMV's bill of sale form in New York?

The DMV's MV-912 is commonly used for vehicle transfers and sales-tax purposes; this template captures the same core details. Check dmv.ny.gov for what they require at registration.

Is sales tax handled on the bill of sale?

The sale price on the bill of sale is used to calculate sales tax at registration, but tax itself is paid to the DMV. Verify current rules at dmv.ny.gov.
