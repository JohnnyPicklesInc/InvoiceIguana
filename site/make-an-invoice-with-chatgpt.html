What the assistant sees

🔒

The assistant sees what you tell it in the chat, under its own privacy policy. Invoice Iguana never sees the conversation: the invoice details travel inside the link, in the part after the # that browsers never send to a server, and the page rebuilds the invoice in your browser.

Questions

Can ChatGPT make an invoice PDF?

Not directly as a reliable file, but with this it does not need to: open the link ChatGPT gives you, tap Print / Save as PDF, and choose Save as PDF. You get a clean one-page invoice with no chat around it.

Do I need a ChatGPT account?

No account is needed for Invoice Iguana. ChatGPT itself has worked for us without logging in, but logged-in ChatGPT is more reliable at reading web pages. Claude and Gemini work the same way.

Can the AI get the total wrong?

The assistant only writes the items, quantities, prices and tax rate into the link. The invoice page computes every subtotal, tax amount and total itself, so arithmetic never comes from the AI.

How do I get paid from the invoice?

Tell the assistant your PayPal.me name, $Cashtag or Venmo username, or paste a Stripe or Square payment link. The invoice shows a Pay this invoice button. You can also add Zelle or bank details as payment instructions.

Can it make quotes, receipts or split a bill?

Yes. Ask for a quote or estimate, a receipt, or to split a restaurant bill — the same page explains each format to the assistant.
