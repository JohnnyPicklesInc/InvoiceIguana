Cash receipt FAQ

What should a cash receipt include?

Who received the payment, who paid, the amount, what it was for, the date, and "paid in cash". A receipt number and signature line add a paper trail for cash, which has no bank record.

Why give a receipt for a cash payment?

Cash leaves no automatic record, so a receipt is the proof both sides rely on. It protects you against "I already paid" disputes and helps with bookkeeping and taxes.

Is a handwritten cash receipt valid?

Yes, but a clear itemized receipt with your business name and a number looks more professional and is easier to reconcile. This template gives you that in seconds.
