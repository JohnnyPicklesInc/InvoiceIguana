Fencing quote & estimate FAQ

What should a fencing estimate include?

Your business, the property, the fence type and linear footage, the per-foot installed rate, gates, old-fence removal, permits if needed, the valid-until date, deposit, and total.

How is fencing quoted — per foot or per job?

Per linear foot for the run, with gates and removal as separate lines. Terrain and post spacing affect the rate, so note the assumptions.

Who pulls the permit for a fence?

It varies — say in the estimate whether the permit and any HOA approval are included or the homeowner’s responsibility.
