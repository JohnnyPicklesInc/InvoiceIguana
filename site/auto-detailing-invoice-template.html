Auto Detailing invoice FAQ

What should an auto detailing invoice include?

Your business name, the customer, the vehicle (make/model/size), the package, any add-ons, the date, tax, and total. Vehicle size often changes the price, so note it.

Should detailing be priced by package or vehicle size?

Both — packages (interior, exterior, full) with pricing tiered by vehicle size (car, SUV, truck). List the package and any add-ons as separate lines.

How do I invoice mobile detailing?

Same as a shop, plus an optional travel/trip fee as its own line. Save the client and reuse the invoice for repeat customers.
