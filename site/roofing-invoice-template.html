Roofing invoice FAQ

What should a roofing invoice include?

Your business and license/insurance info, the customer and property address, the roof size (in squares), materials, tear-off/disposal, labor, any deposit, tax, total, and the workmanship warranty. Reference the estimate or insurance claim number.

How is roofing priced — per square or per job?

Roofers price by the "square" (100 sq ft), then add tear-off, underlayment, flashing, and disposal. This example shows a per-square replacement plus itemized extras; you can adjust for repairs.

How do I invoice a roofing insurance job?

Itemize to match the adjuster’s scope and reference the claim number. Bill the deductible to the homeowner and note any supplements. Keeping line items close to the estimate speeds approval.
