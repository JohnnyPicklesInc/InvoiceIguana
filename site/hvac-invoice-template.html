HVAC invoice FAQ

What should an HVAC invoice include?

Your company and license/EPA info, the customer and property address, equipment with model where relevant, labor hours and rate, refrigerant/materials, tax, total, and terms. For maintenance, note the system serviced and any recommendations.

How do I invoice an HVAC maintenance visit vs. an install?

A tune-up is usually a single flat line (e.g. "Seasonal AC maintenance"), while an install itemizes equipment, materials, and labor hours. This template does both — start from the AI prompt and adjust.

Should I put the equipment model number on the invoice?

For installs and warranty claims, yes — add it to the line item or the notes. It helps the customer with rebates and future service.
