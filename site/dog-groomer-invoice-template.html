Dog Groomer invoice FAQ

What should a dog grooming invoice include?

Your business name, the client and pet (name/breed/size), the groom package, add-ons, the date, total, and payment terms. Size and coat often change the price, so note them.

Should grooming be priced by breed or size?

Usually by size and coat type, with add-ons (nails, teeth, de-shedding) as separate lines. This template itemizes the groom plus add-ons.

How do I invoice recurring grooming clients?

Save the client and duplicate the last invoice each visit — same pet, same package — and just update the date.
