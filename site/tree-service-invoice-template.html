Tree Service invoice FAQ

What should a tree service invoice include?

Your business, license/insurance, the customer and property address, each service (removal, trimming, stump grinding), cleanup/haul-away, tax, and total. Note if permits or utility coordination were involved.

How is tree work priced?

By the job, based on size, access, and hazards. Big removals, stump grinding, and cleanup are typically separate lines — that’s how this example is set up.

Should cleanup and hauling be a separate line?

Yes — separating debris haul-away and cleanup from the removal itself keeps the invoice clear and lets customers opt out if they want to keep the wood.
