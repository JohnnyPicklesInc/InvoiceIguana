Pool Service invoice FAQ

What should a pool service invoice include?

Your business name, the customer and property address, the service plan or visit, chemicals and parts, the billing period, tax, and total. Note the number of visits covered for monthly plans.

How do I invoice a monthly pool maintenance plan?

Bill a flat monthly rate plus any extras (filter cleans, repairs) as separate lines. Save the client and duplicate the invoice each month to rebill.

Should chemicals be billed separately?

Some plans include chemicals; others bill them as used. Listing them as a line keeps pricing transparent when usage varies.
