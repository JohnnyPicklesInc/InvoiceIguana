Bathroom Remodel quote & estimate FAQ

What should a bathroom remodel estimate include?

The scope by phase (demo, rough-in, tile, fixtures, finish), material allowances, labor, exclusions, the valid-until date, deposit and payment schedule, and the total. Allowances prevent surprises when the client picks finishes.

What is an allowance on a remodel quote?

A budgeted amount for items the client will select later (tile, fixtures, vanity). If they choose pricier options, the difference is a change order. Note allowances clearly in the estimate.

How much deposit for a remodel?

Commonly 25–35% to start, with progress payments by milestone. State the deposit and schedule so cash flow is agreed up front.
