Massage Therapist invoice FAQ

What should a massage therapy invoice include?

Your name and license, the client, the session type and length, add-ons, the date, total, and payment terms. If a client needs it for insurance/HSA, add your credentials and a service code.

Can clients use a massage invoice for HSA/FSA?

Often, with a clear itemized invoice showing your license, the service, and the date. Include those details and any required code in the notes.

How do I invoice a package of sessions?

Bill the package as one line (e.g. "5-session package") or itemize sessions. Save the client to rebill or track remaining sessions in the notes.
