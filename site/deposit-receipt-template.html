Deposit receipt FAQ

What should a deposit receipt include?

Who received it, who paid, the amount, what the deposit is for, the date, the payment method, and the terms for holding or refunding it. A reference to the lease or contract ties it to the agreement.

Why give a receipt for a security deposit?

It proves the amount and date the deposit was paid — essential when it’s time to return it or resolve a dispute. Many jurisdictions also require written acknowledgment.

Is a deposit receipt the same as a rent receipt?

No — a deposit is held and often refundable, while rent is a payment for occupancy. Keep them as separate receipts so the records are clear.
