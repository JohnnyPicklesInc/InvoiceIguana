Trailer bill of sale FAQ

What should a trailer bill of sale include?

Seller and buyer, the trailer (year, type, size, VIN, axle count), price, date, payment, an as-is clause, and signatures. Homemade trailers may need an extra state form to get a VIN assigned.

Does a small utility trailer need a title?

It varies — some states title all trailers, others exempt small ones under a weight limit. Check your DMV; a bill of sale is still worth keeping either way.

Can I sell a homemade trailer with a bill of sale?

Yes, but the buyer may need to get a state-assigned VIN and inspection to register it. Note it's homemade in the description.
